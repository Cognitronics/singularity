BUREAU MANUAL

Classified · Division S of the Bureau of Investigation · For authorised agents only

1 · The situation

It is 1920. Manhattan exists not once but in countless parallel universes, and they are drifting into each other. Sooner or later only one version will survive the Singularity. You lead a secret government division nobody knows exists. Your task: make sure your Manhattan is the one that remains.

A universe holds together through anchors – ten landmarks of the city that stabilise reality. Whoever holds enough of them keeps their universe stable; all others collapse into the ether.

2 · How to win

  • Have 6 of the 10 anchors active at the end of an epoch.
  • The game runs for 48 turns, divided into 6 epochs of 8 turns each.
  • You lose if instability reaches 100 % or time runs out with fewer than 6 anchors.

3 · A turn

  1. Click a district on the map. It lights up, the others turn grey. The panel on the right shows what the district earns and what you can build there. Click the water to see all of Manhattan again.
  2. Build facilities, use psy actions, choose a research goal and activate anchors whose condition you meet.
  3. Press End turn. Income, resources and events are calculated and reported in the newspaper.

4 · Resources

$ MoneyIncome from all districts every turn, plus $1,000 from the federal black budget. Pays for facilities and their upkeep. If it drops below zero, loyalty suffers.
Power (MW)Generators produce it, most facilities consume it. If there is not enough, some facilities shut down.
◆ CrystalsEther crystals from drills. Needed for advanced facilities and to activate anchors.
PsyMental energy from laboratories and radio towers. Spent on psy actions.
ResearchPoints from laboratories. Unlock technologies or locate hidden anchors.

5 · The city

Four values describe the mood of all of Manhattan. They are the same for every district.

ProsperityHow wealthy the city is. Multiplies your income. Drifts back towards 55 %.
LoyaltyHow much people trust the authorities. Above 50 % your control grows, below it erodes. Falls when the press gets curious or wages go unpaid.
UnrestHow restless people are. Lowers income and rises with every facility. Above 60 % unions strike in the Lower East Side, above 70 % the police may raid a facility.
ControlHow firmly the bureau holds the city. Multiplies income, is required for some anchors, grows with every district that has a facility.

Income of a district = base × Prosperity × Control × (1 − Unrest ÷ 2)
Districts without a facility pay only 30 %.

Pick a branch in the Research tab; your laboratories work on it automatically. Levels cost 8, 16 and 28 points.

Instead of a branch you can choose anchor search: your ether cartographers then use research points to locate a hidden anchor.

9 · Anchors

At the start you know none of the ten anchors. You find them through anchor search or when a famous scientist joins the bureau – up to three times per game someone like Nikola Tesla or Marie Curie offers to work for you in secret and brings knowledge of one anchor.

A found anchor shows its condition. Once you meet it, you can activate the anchor with crystals.

  • Fixed anchors stay yours once activated.
  • Roaming anchors are checked at the end of every epoch. If their condition is no longer met, you lose them.

10 · Psy actions

Psy actions affect all of Manhattan. Each one draws a little press attention and adds 0.5 % instability.

Calm5 psy · −6 unrest
Inspire8 psy · +6 loyalty
Whisper10 psy · +4 control
Spending Spree12 psy · +20 % income for 2 turns, then unrest rises
Forget15 psy · press attention −30

11 · Instability

The bar at the top left shows how close your universe is to falling apart. Drills in Central Park, Tesla generators, psy actions, portal trades and ether storms raise it; stabilizers lower it.

  • From 50 %: doppelgängers appear, unrest rises.
  • From 80 %: control slips away.
  • At 100 %: your Manhattan dissolves – game over.

12 · Press, events and weather

Too much secret activity makes the newspapers curious. Above 60 press attention, loyalty drops every turn. Fog and the psy action Forget help.

History runs on: Prohibition, mayoral elections, strikes, heat waves, ether storms, a stock market boom – and at some point Black Thursday.

Weather: rain lowers unrest, fog lowers press attention, storms cut power output by 15 %.

13 · Tips for new agents

  • Build a generator early – without power nothing runs.
  • A facility in every district pays off: more income and more control.
  • Keep an eye on unrest. Calm is cheap.
  • Start the anchor search in time – anchors need several turns to find and meet.
  • A stabilizer in time saves your universe.
